Warfarin — official leaflet excerpt (prototype)

4.1 Therapeutic indications
Prevention of venous thrombosis and pulmonary embolism.

4.2 Posology
Once daily, usually in the evening at 18:00. INR monitoring is required.

4.5 Interactions
Major interaction with NSAIDs (Ibuprofen) and Aspirin. Caution with high-dose Paracetamol.

4.8 Undesirable effects
Bleeding and easy bruising.
