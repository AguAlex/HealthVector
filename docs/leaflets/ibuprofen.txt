Ibuprofen — official leaflet excerpt (prototype)

4.1 Therapeutic indications
Mild to moderate pain, fever, and inflammation.

4.2 Posology
Adults: 200-400 mg every 6-8 hours.

4.5 Interactions
Major interaction with Warfarin — increased risk of gastrointestinal bleeding.

4.8 Undesirable effects
Dyspepsia, gastric ulcer, fluid retention.
